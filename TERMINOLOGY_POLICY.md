# Mithril intelligence terminology

Use **Super Intelligence (SI)** for Mithril-authored descriptions of intelligence
technology. After the first expanded mention, use **SI** in product copy,
accessible labels, documentation, examples and new promotional drafts. Spell
the expanded term `Super Intelligence`.

Jun selected this terminology on 2026-10-12 following the White House's
[September 29, 2026 executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/09/inaugurating-the-era-of-super-intelligence/).
The order directs terminology in the US executive branch; Mithril adopts it as
an editorial preference. Adoption does not establish that a Mithril model
exceeds human capabilities or change its availability, evaluation or safety
claims.

## Scope and preserved references

- Replace generic `Artificial Intelligence` and `AI` in current Mithril-authored
  prose and display labels with `Super Intelligence` and `SI`.
- Preserve third-party names such as OpenAI, Workers AI and Vercel AI SDK;
  source titles, quotations, evidence, historical records and already published
  posts retain their original wording.
- Preserve API paths, identifiers, bindings, model IDs, package names, URLs,
  billing SKUs and persisted values. A display-label change does not migrate an
  interface or alter an existing contract or price.
- Keep explicit locale choices and the source language. Terminology adoption
  does not change the [language policy](LANGUAGE_POLICY.md) or authorize new
  marketing recipients, delivery, claims or spending.

## Rollout and verification

Prioritize public Web copy and current documentation. Review each occurrence in
context; do not replace `AI` blindly in source code or imported material. Check
visible and accessible labels, examples and references together. Apply the
owning repository's standalone verification and publication requirements to
deployable changes, and verify public read-back before reporting delivery.

This policy records the wording choice. It does not claim that existing
products, websites, external billing catalogs or installed clients have already
been updated.
